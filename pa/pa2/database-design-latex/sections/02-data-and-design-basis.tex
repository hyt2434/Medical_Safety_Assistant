\subsection{Important Data-Quality Findings}

The logical design preserves incomplete and disputed source information rather than deleting it:
\begin{itemize}
  \item 8,741 of 10,670 medication candidates are Level-2 qualified, while 1,929 are not.
  \item 9,313 medications have no explicitly supported route; the system must not infer oral administration.
  \item 1,540 ingredient identities are auto-verified source-local labels, while 400 remain source-asserted only.
  \item 2,657 medication components have no accepted ingredient identifier, including 662 intentionally unresolved components.
  \item all 5,614 indication rows are candidates; no row is currently an approved product indication.
  \item all 5,675 DDInter mappings are candidates with \status{accepted=false} and \status{operative=false}.
\end{itemize}

Consequently, null foreign keys and review states are meaningful business states, not merely data defects. The physical design must distinguish ``unknown or awaiting review'' from ``not applicable'' and from ``rejected.''

\subsection{Data Not Present in the Current CSV Package}

The current six files do not contain interaction pairs, interaction severity, or evidence for each pair. Therefore, they cannot directly populate \db{DRUG_INTERACTIONS}, \db{DRUG_INTERACTION_PARTICIPANTS}, or \db{INTERACTION_EVIDENCE}. Module 3 currently expects a separate DDInter interaction file containing \db{DDInterID_A}, \db{Drug_A}, \db{DDInterID_B}, \db{Drug_B}, and \db{Level}.

The absence of a pair from a loaded interaction dataset must never be interpreted as proof that no interaction exists. Runtime results must retain the dataset release and evidence context used for the assessment.

\subsection{Design Principles}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Trace every decision to evidence.} Source records, hashes, validation results, reviewer decisions, and evidence links are retained.
  \item \textbf{Separate candidate from operative data.} Imported or machine-generated mappings cannot affect patient-facing safety results until approved and released.
  \item \textbf{Make assessments reproducible.} A safety assessment references an immutable medication-list snapshot and an explicit dataset release.
  \item \textbf{Separate clinical care from data governance.} HCP care privileges and medical-data-review privileges are independently assigned.
  \item \textbf{Apply least privilege.} Technical support handles tickets and diagnostic context, not clinical conclusions; patient access is constrained to owned records; HCP access is constrained by care relationships.
  \item \textbf{Prefer deterministic safety logic.} Drug interaction matching uses reviewed identifiers and relational joins. Statistical similarity is not an authoritative safety decision.
\end{enumerate}
