\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Purpose}

This Software Development Plan defines how the Medication Safety Assistant
project will be organized, executed, monitored, and controlled. It provides a
shared working agreement for the project team and gives the instructors and
teaching assistants a basis for reviewing the team's plan and progress. The
document covers the initial lifecycle plan, team responsibilities, iteration
objectives, planned releases, requirements management, and risk management.

This is the initial plan produced during the Inception phase. It will be
reviewed at the end of each sprint and updated when scope, risks, estimates, or
course milestones change.

\subsection{Scope}

This plan applies to the design and development of the Medication Safety
Assistant, a responsive Web application that helps patients understand
potential medication-related risks and follow confirmed medication schedules.
The planned product accepts printed or digital Vietnamese prescriptions,
manual medication entries, typed requests, and voice input. OCR converts
prescription images into editable text, speech-to-text converts recorded speech
into an editable transcript, and NLP identifies the user's intent and relevant
medication information. It supports user verification, medication
normalization, drug--drug interaction assessment, safety guidance, professional
review of high-risk or uncertain cases, reminders, adherence tracking, and
recording of possible adverse drug reactions.

The plan covers the three RUP phases required for the course---Inception,
Elaboration, and Construction---implemented through five two-week sprints. It
does not cover production deployment to a healthcare organization, integration
with real clinical systems, or operation as a regulated clinical decision
system.
