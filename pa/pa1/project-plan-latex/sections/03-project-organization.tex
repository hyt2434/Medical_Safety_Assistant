\section{Project Organization}

\subsection{Organizational Structure}

The project is executed by a self-managing team of three students. Hoàng Yến
Trang is the Team Leader and Project Manager and is the primary contact with the
instructors and teaching assistants. Planning decisions are discussed by the
whole team. A decision that changes product scope, medical-safety behavior, or
a committed sprint objective requires team review and must be recorded in the
project workspace.

Work is organized as a shared product backlog and five sprint backlogs. Each
work item has one accountable owner, acceptance criteria, and a tracked status.
Another member reviews safety-critical requirements, code, and test evidence
before the item is accepted. The proposed role allocation below is an initial
working arrangement and may be adjusted at sprint retrospectives according to
workload and demonstrated skills.
