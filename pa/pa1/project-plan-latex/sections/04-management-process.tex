\subsection{Project Monitoring and Control}

\subsubsection{Requirements Management}

The Vision Document defines the main project requirements.
Detailed requirements are managed through use cases
and GitHub Issues. Each requirement has a priority,
assigned owner, and acceptance criteria.
Safety-related requirements must also cover possible
errors and missing data.

Requirement changes are recorded in GitHub Issues
and reviewed based on their impact on project scope,
safety, and schedule. The Project Manager and the
responsible team member review each change.
Major changes require agreement from the whole team.
Approved changes are updated in the related documents
and GitHub Projects.

Requirements and task progress are tracked using
GitHub Projects and reviewed during each sprint.
A requirement is considered complete when its
acceptance criteria are met and verified through
appropriate reviews or tests.

\subsubsection{Risk Management}

Project risks are identified during planning,
development, and testing. Each risk is ranked
by likelihood and impact, with an assigned owner
and a mitigation plan.

The team reviews risks weekly and tracks related
issues using GitHub Issues. Risks are updated
based on project progress and test results.

\begin{longtable}{|L{0.07\textwidth}|L{0.10\textwidth}|L{0.27\textwidth}|L{0.25\textwidth}|L{0.12\textwidth}|L{0.09\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Ranking} &
\textbf{Description and Impact} &
\textbf{Mitigation / Contingency} &
\textbf{Owner} & \textbf{Status}\\ \hline

R-01 & High
& Incorrect medication identification or ingredient
mapping may lead to wrong safety results.
& Verify medication details, review mappings,
and keep unclear matches unresolved.
& Long & Open\\ \hline

R-02 & High
& OCR errors may cause incorrect medication names,
strengths, or schedules.
& Require user confirmation and provide
manual correction or entry.
& Trang & Open\\ \hline

R-03 & High
& The NLP chatbot may produce incorrect or
unsupported medical explanations.
& Base explanations on verified safety results,
limit AI decisions, and test unsafe responses.
& Long & Open\\ \hline

R-04 & High
& Voice recognition may misunderstand medication
names or instructions.
& Show editable transcripts, require confirmation,
and provide Manual Input as a fallback.
& Thành & Open\\ \hline

R-05 & High
& Limited medication data, DDInter coverage,
or data licensing may affect safety checks.
& Use permitted data, record source limits,
and support replaceable data sources.
& Long & Open\\ \hline

R-06 & High
& Users may believe that no detected interaction
means the medications are completely safe.
& Show clear risk levels, data limitations,
and warnings when information is incomplete.
& Trang / Long & Open\\ \hline

R-07 & Medium
& Sensitive medication and personal information
may be exposed.
& Use test data, limit access,
protect user information, and avoid
storing sensitive data unnecessarily.
& Whole team & Open\\ \hline

R-08 & High
& The reminder safety gate may fail to block
new reminders for drugs with major interactions
or may block unrelated medications.
& Test the gate for each affected medication.
Keep existing reminders unchanged and require
HCP review before activating affected new reminders.
& Thành & Open\\ \hline

R-09 & Medium
& Limited time and uneven workload may delay
project completion.
& Prioritize core features, track tasks weekly,
and adjust workloads when needed.
& Trang & Open\\ \hline

R-10 & Medium
& Different module data formats may cause
integration errors.
& Define a common medication data format,
agree on module interfaces, and test integration early.
& Whole team & Open\\ \hline

R-11 & Medium
& Simulated HCP review may not reflect
real medical practice, and corrections may
leave outdated safety results.
& Clearly mark HCP review as simulated.
Record review decisions and reassess
affected data after corrections.
& Trang & Open\\ \hline

R-12 & Medium
& Reminder notifications may be delayed,
missed, or sent more than once.
& Track delivery status, test notification failures,
and prevent duplicate reminders.
& Thành & Open\\ \hline

\end{longtable}
