\section{Introduction}

This Vision Document defines the high-level stakeholder needs, product scope,
and capabilities of the Medication Safety Assistant. It explains the problem
the product addresses, the people who benefit from it, the intended position of
the product, its principal features, and the quality and safety constraints
that govern those features.

The product is a responsive Web prototype for safer medication understanding
and follow-up. It accepts prescription images, manual entries, typed requests,
and voice input. OCR and speech-to-text produce editable text; NLP identifies
the user's intent and relevant medication information. Only user-verified data
continues to medicine identification and safety assessment. Vietnamese products
and active ingredients are identified using medicine-registration data from the
Drug Administration of Vietnam (DAV), then mapped to DDInter 2.0 for supported
drug--drug interaction checks. The product communicates findings and
limitations, distinguishes routine, review-required, and emergency outcomes,
and provides immediate escalation guidance for red flags. It does not diagnose,
prescribe, or independently change treatment.

This is an initial Inception-phase vision. Detailed use cases, supplementary
specifications, implementation decisions, and acceptance tests will refine it
during Elaboration and Construction.
