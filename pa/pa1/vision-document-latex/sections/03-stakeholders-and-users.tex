\subsection{User Environment}

The product is used through modern desktop and mobile Web browsers. Patients
and caregivers may use it at home, after receiving a prescription, while
organizing daily medicines, or before consulting a healthcare professional.
They may have only a paper or digital Vietnamese prescription, may know a
medicine by brand name, or may prefer to speak rather than type. A camera is
needed for prescription capture, a microphone and browser permission are needed
for voice input, and notification permission is needed for browser-supported
reminders. Manual entry remains available when these capabilities are absent.

Use may occur under time pressure, with background noise, poor lighting,
unstable connectivity, small screens, or limited health and digital literacy.
Therefore, OCR extractions and voice transcripts must be editable, medical
terms must use plain language, warnings must not rely on color alone, and the
system must preserve an explicit incomplete/unassessed state. The prototype
uses synthetic or de-identified data and is not integrated with hospital,
pharmacy, electronic health-record, or real professional-review systems.

Healthcare professional reviewers use a structured Web view, preferably on a
desktop or tablet, to inspect verified medicines, source and mapping status,
detected risks, stated limitations, symptoms, and review requests.

\subsection{Summary of Key Stakeholder or User Needs}

\begin{longtable}{|L{0.21\textwidth}|L{0.09\textwidth}|L{0.20\textwidth}|L{0.20\textwidth}|L{0.20\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Need} & \textbf{Priority} & \textbf{Concerns / cause} & \textbf{Current solution} & \textbf{Proposed solution}\\ \hline
Enter medicines with low effort & High & Long prescriptions, spelling, Vietnamese brands, accessibility, or typing difficulty. & Manually copy information or ask another person. & OCR, manual, typed, and voice input with one editable verification flow.\\ \hline
Prevent input errors from becoming safety results & High & OCR and speech recognition may alter names, strengths, or schedules. & Re-read the prescription without systematic validation. & Require correction and explicit confirmation; flag low-confidence or unresolved fields.\\ \hline
Provide enough safety context & High & Interaction significance can depend on the complete medicine list, dose, route, allergies, age, pregnancy, symptoms, and kidney or liver impairment. & Users may omit information or search several sources independently. & Ask for required context, show what is missing, and keep the case incomplete or unassessed until sufficient information is available.\\ \hline
Identify local products and active ingredients & High & Brand names can hide related ingredients or differ from international names. & Search labels or multiple websites manually. & Match verified information to DAV records and retain source provenance.\\ \hline
Understand potential medicine interactions & High & Users may not recognize a risky combination or its significance. & Search the Web or ask a professional when available. & Map ingredients to DDInter 2.0 and present supported interaction findings and severity.\\ \hline
Understand uncertainty and coverage & High & A missing match or absent record may be mistaken for safety. & Results from different sources may be inconsistent or unexplained. & Distinguish detected, no known interaction in available data, incomplete, and unassessed states.\\ \hline
Know when to seek professional advice & High & Users may overreact, ignore a warning, delay emergency care, or change treatment independently. & Contact a doctor or pharmacist without a structured summary. & Separate emergency red flags from review-required cases, provide immediate urgent guidance when needed, and prepare uncertain or unresolved cases for review.\\ \hline
Follow a confirmed medication schedule & Medium & Users forget doses or misread instructions. & Paper notes, alarms, or generic reminder applications. & Offer reminders as an explicit opt-in only after the prescription schedule has been verified and confirmed, then record taken/missed status.\\ \hline
Record possible adverse reactions & Medium & Symptoms may be forgotten or described inconsistently. & Personal notes or memory. & Record symptoms as possible, not confirmed, adverse reactions for follow-up and review.\\ \hline
Protect sensitive health information & High & Images, audio, transcripts, medicine lists, and symptoms are sensitive. & Store information across uncontrolled notes or messaging applications. & Minimize collection and retention, obtain permission, restrict access, and avoid real patient data in the prototype.\\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Alternatives and Competition}

\begin{longtable}{|L{0.23\textwidth}|L{0.23\textwidth}|L{0.24\textwidth}|L{0.20\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Alternative} & \textbf{Strengths} & \textbf{Weaknesses} & \textbf{Our differentiation}\\ \hline
Direct consultation with a doctor or pharmacist & Qualified clinical judgment and individualized advice. & Access, time, and cost may be limited; users may arrive with incomplete medicine information. & The product does not replace consultation; it prepares a verified, structured summary and signals cases needing review.\\ \hline
General Web search or general-purpose AI chatbot & Convenient and flexible natural-language access. & Source quality and coverage may be unclear; generated responses may imply unsupported certainty. & NLP is limited to input understanding and explanation of structured system findings, with explicit provenance and uncertainty.\\ \hline
Standalone interaction checker & Focused interaction lookup and often broad international drug coverage. & May require users to know normalized ingredient names; may not recognize Vietnamese product names or support intake and follow-up. & DAV-based local product identification precedes DDInter mapping and is connected to verification, guidance, and follow-up.\\ \hline
Medication reminder application & Useful alarms and adherence tracking. & May not verify prescription information or check severe interactions before creating reminders. & Reminders use confirmed schedules and apply a safety gate to affected medicines.\\ \hline
Paper prescription, notes, or phone alarms & Familiar, inexpensive, and usable offline. & Manual transcription, scattered information, limited risk explanation, and weak traceability. & A unified workflow connects capture, verification, safety assessment, guidance, reminders, and follow-up.\\ \hline
\end{longtable}
