
\section{Project Organization}

\subsection{Organizational Structure}

The project is developed by a team of three students,
led by Hoàng Yến Trang as the Project Manager and Team Leader.
The Team Leader manages project activities and communicates
with instructors and teaching assistants.

Tasks are assigned based on each member's responsibilities
and tracked using a shared project management tool.
Major decisions about project scope and safety requirements
are discussed by the whole team.
