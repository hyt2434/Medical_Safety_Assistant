\subsection{Product Perspective}

\PAChange{The Medication Safety Assistant is developed
with a long-term vision of making medication use
safer and healthcare information more accessible
to people worldwide. The project aims to help
users understand potential medication risks and
follow confirmed medication schedules.}

\PAChange{The current product is a standalone,
responsive Web research prototype focused on
Vietnamese medication information. It supports
medication intake, safety checks, simulated HCP
review, reminders, and adherence tracking.
It does not diagnose, prescribe, independently
change treatment, or connect to real healthcare
systems.}

The system consists of five main modules:

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Medication Intake and Intent Recognition:}
    Identifies user intent through UI selection or
    NLP, and collects medication information
    through OCR, NLP Chatbot, Voice/STT, and
    Manual Input.
    \PAChange{Users review, correct, and confirm
    recorded details before safety assessment
    or reminder activation.}

    \item \textbf{Medication Identification:}
    Identifies Vietnamese medications and active
    ingredients using permitted data sources.
    Uncertain matches remain unresolved.

    \item \textbf{Medication Safety Assessment:}
    \PAChange{Checks supported drug--drug interactions
    using available DDInter 2.0 data and verified
    ingredient mappings. It also checks duplicate
    active ingredients when enough information is
    available. Results show detected risks, severity
    when supported, and unassessed or failed checks;
    missing findings are not proof of safety.}

    \item \textbf{HCP Review:}
    \PAChange{Allows patients to request simulated
    review of saved safety cases with permission
    to share selected information. Authorized HCPs
    can examine findings, request more information,
    exchange messages with patients, and record
    traceable review outcomes.}

    \item \textbf{Medication Reminders and Adherence:}
    \PAChange{Supports reminders from verified
    prescription schedules or recorded HCP
    instructions, applies the new-reminder safety
    gate, and tracks dose status and adherence.}

\end{enumerate}

\PAChange{The usual safety workflow is medication
intake, identification, and assessment. Patients
may request HCP review or set up eligible reminders
when needed; these are not mandatory steps in
all user journeys. Unidentified medicines and
unsupported checks remain clearly marked,
not treated as safe.}

\PAChange{A reminder schedule must match an existing
prescription or recorded HCP instruction and be
confirmed by the patient. Any proposed change to
prescribed timing requires recorded, simulated
HCP confirmation before activation.}

\PAChange{Detected severe drug interactions trigger
immediate warnings. New reminders for affected
medicines remain Pending HCP Review; unrelated
eligible reminders may proceed. The system does
not automatically stop prescribed medications
or existing reminders. HCP review in this
prototype is simulated, not clinical approval.}

\PAChange{Other current features include medication
and safety history, a multi-turn AI chatbot for
supported medication questions, and patient health
summaries. Patients review summaries before saving
or sharing them with authorized HCPs. The chatbot
is separate from patient--HCP case conversations.
Dataset review, user and role management, and
technical support provide restricted supporting
workflows.}

\PAChange{\textbf{Future Enhancements (outside the
current prototype scope):} Consultation preparation,
medication record comparison, explanations of
confirmed instructions, and a Personal Medication
Inventory for stock and expiration tracking may
be considered later. Other possible extensions
include user-reported suspected side effects,
general disease information, and more advanced
urgent-care guidance. These features require
additional reliable data, validation, and safety
controls and are not included in the current
use cases or UI scope.}

% User confirmation
Assumption
& \PAChange{Patients can review, correct, and confirm
recorded medication details against their provided
source before safety checks or reminder activation.}
& \PAChange{Unconfirmed information cannot be used
for those actions. Confirmation does not prove
medical accuracy or medicine identity.}
\\ \hline

% Vietnamese medication data
Dependency
& \PAChange{Permitted Vietnamese medication data and
supported active-ingredient mappings are available
for medication identification.}
& \PAChange{Unidentified medicines remain unresolved.
Checks requiring their identity are unassessed;
other supported checks may proceed.}
\\ \hline

% Interaction data
Dependency
& \PAChange{Usable DDInter 2.0 interaction data and
verified ingredient mappings are available under
applicable source terms and permissions.}
& \PAChange{Unsupported drug pairs remain unassessed.
Independent checks, such as duplicate-ingredient
checks, may still run when supported.}
\\ \hline

% OCR
Dependency
& OCR can extract medication information
from clear printed or digital prescriptions.
& Users must correct extraction errors
or switch to Manual Input.
\\ \hline

% Speech-to-text
Dependency
& Speech-to-text can convert voice input
into editable text.
& Users can correct the transcript
or switch to typed or manual input.
\\ \hline

% NLP
Dependency
& NLP can identify user intents and
medication information from text.
& Users must clarify unclear requests
or select a function manually.
\\ \hline

% Simulated HCP workflow
Assumption
& \PAChange{Authorized HCP accounts can access
patient-approved cases for simulated review
and case-based conversations.}
& \PAChange{When no reviewer is available,
requests remain Pending. No case is claimed
reviewed, and required reminder holds remain.}
\\ \hline

% Verified schedules
Assumption
& \PAChange{Existing prescriptions or recorded
HCP instructions provide verifiable medication
schedules.}
& \PAChange{Unverified schedules cannot activate
new reminders. Proposed changes to prescribed
timing require simulated HCP confirmation.}
\\ \hline

% Browser capabilities
Dependency
& Web browsers support camera, microphone,
and reminder notifications when needed.
& \PAChange{Manual input remains available when
OCR or voice cannot be used. Eligible schedules
remain viewable if notifications are unavailable.}
\\ \hline

% Role-based access
Dependency
& \PAChange{User accounts, role permissions, and
case-level sharing controls are available for
authorized access.}
& \PAChange{Restricted features and private
patient information remain inaccessible
when permissions cannot be verified.}
\\ \hline

% Persistent records and review history
\PAChange{Dependency}
& \PAChange{Reliable storage is available for
medication records, assessments, HCP conversations,
review outcomes, and relevant activity history.}
& \PAChange{Failed saves are reported; unsaved
information is not treated as confirmed,
reviewed, or successfully shared.}
\\ \hline

% Dataset evidence and traceability
\PAChange{Dependency}
& \PAChange{Medication records and mappings retain
available source references and dataset review
status for quality checks and traceability.}
& \PAChange{Missing evidence is reported, and
unsupported claims are not presented as
clinically validated.}
\\ \hline

\end{longtable}
