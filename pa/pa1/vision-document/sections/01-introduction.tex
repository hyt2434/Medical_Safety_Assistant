
\section{Introduction}

This Vision Document describes the high-level needs,
scope, and main features of the Medication Safety Assistant.
It explains the problems faced by patients taking multiple
medications, the benefits of the proposed solution,
and the needs of its target users.

The Medication Safety Assistant is a responsive Web
application that helps patients identify potential
medication risks and follow confirmed medication
schedules. It supports prescription images, natural-language
requests, voice input, and manual forms. The system provides
medication safety checks, warnings, simulated healthcare
professional (HCP) review, and medication reminders.
It does not replace professional medical advice or
independently prescribe or change medications.

This initial Vision Document will be updated as
requirements are reviewed and refined during development.
