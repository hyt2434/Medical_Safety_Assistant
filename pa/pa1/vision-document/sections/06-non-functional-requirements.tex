
\section{Non-Functional Requirements}

The following requirements define the main
quality goals and constraints of the
Medication Safety Assistant.

\begin{longtable}{|L{0.13\textwidth}|L{0.68\textwidth}|L{0.10\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{ID} &
\textbf{Requirement} &
\textbf{Priority}\\ \hline

NFR-01
& \textbf{Performance:}
The system should respond quickly to common
requests. Medication safety checks should
finish within 10 seconds for at least 90\%
of standard test cases under normal conditions.
Longer tasks should show processing status.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-02
& \textbf{Reliability and Fault Tolerance:}
The system should handle errors and missing
data without crashing. If OCR, Voice/STT,
or NLP fails, users can correct information
or use another input method. Failed safety
checks must not be reported as safe.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-03
& \textbf{Medication Safety:}
The system must clearly show uncertain or
incomplete results. It must not independently
change prescribed treatment. New reminders
affected by severe interactions require
HCP review before activation.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-04
& \textbf{Security and Privacy:}
The system must protect user accounts and
medication records. Patients can access
their own information, while HCPs can only
access cases they are allowed to review.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-05
& \textbf{Usability and Accessibility:}
The interface should be simple, responsive,
and easy to use. Medication warnings must
be clear, readable, and understandable,
including for users with limited
technical experience.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-06
& \textbf{Platform Compatibility:}
The application should work on modern
desktop and mobile Web browsers.
Camera, microphone, and notification
features depend on browser support
and user permissions.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-07
& \textbf{Data Integrity and History:}
The system should keep medication records,
assessment results, and review history
accurate and consistent. Past results
must be clearly distinguished from
current assessments.
& High
\\ \hline

NFR-08
& \textbf{Maintainability and Extensibility:}
The system should use a modular design
to support updates, new data sources,
and future medication safety features
without major changes to existing modules.
& Medium
\\ \hline

NFR-09
& \textbf{Documentation:}
The project should provide basic setup
instructions, user guidance, and
documentation of system limitations.
& Medium
\\ \hline

\end{longtable}
